#### Interactive effect for Instagram/Facebook camera

Creating interactive AR camera effects allows me to interact with various people and the world. It feels like creating a work with the user together because you never expect how and where the user will use this effect. Here are the filters that I made for my own hobby.

Scan the QRcode to try it out!

Made by Meta Spark Studio

</br></br>

##### Escape from Myself

“Every man is his own worst enemy.”

When user opens the mouth, the giant user will emit laser from his/ her eyes, and the small user will speed up to escape.

A little eating game.

In 10 seconds, eat the fresh donut or the rot donut to get some random effects on your face! Don't forget to turn on the sound.
