My bachelor's program is about technology education for young people. It is very interesting for me to design teaching materials for people to learn STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Mathematics) knowledge. Here are some projects about this topic:

#### Robotic Arm DIY kit

This is a STEM teaching material. The purpose is to help technology teachers to teach more efficiently while encourage students to learn relevant knowledge.

- 2018.02-2019.02
- Individual


[Detail of this project](https://haaan.webflow.io/project/robotic-arm-diy-kit)


#### Automatic Coin Bank DIY Kit
{{< postimg url = "education/coin_bank.jpg" alt ="coin bank" imgw ="100%" >}}

</br>

This is a series of electric coin banks with the appearance of Taiwanese protected species, which users can make by themselves. The aim is to disseminate the conservation of animals and their habitats in an interesting and educational way.

- 2018.10-2019.03
- Teamwork
- Project for tourism product design competition (2nd place)

[Detail of this project](https://haaan.webflow.io/project/automatic-coin-bank-diy-kit)


#### AI 2 Robot Car
{{< postimg url = "education/ai2_car.jpg" alt ="ai 2 robot car" imgw ="100%" >}}
</br>

This is an assemble robot car for a board game called ‘’AI 2 Robot City’'. By using an APP to recognize the image of control cards, users control the car moving on the map and play the game.

- 2019.08-2020.09 (Main developed period)
- Teamwork
- Work of research assistant

[Detail of this project](https://haaan.webflow.io/project/ai-2-robot-car)
